Understanding Burnout: A Free Self-Reflection Workbook

By Fouzia Asim · Digital Fattom · Mental Health & Wellbeing

Burnout doesn't announce itself — it builds quietly through exam pressure, overloaded schedules, and days that never quite feel like they end. This free 10-page workbook was built for students and teachers to help recognize burnout early, understand where it's coming from, and take small, realistic steps toward recovery.

What's Inside

  • What burnout actually is (and isn't)
  • A signs & symptoms checklist
  • Root causes — broken down separately for students and teachers
  • What happens if burnout is left unaddressed
  • A guided CBT-style thought reflection exercise
  • A weekly burnout tracker
  • A personal recovery action plan
  • Guidance on when and how to seek professional support
A note before you start: This workbook is an educational self-reflection tool, not a substitute for professional mental health care. If burnout is affecting your daily life, please consider speaking with a counsellor, therapist, or doctor.

Who It's For

Whether you're a student buried under exam pressure or a teacher stretched across a heavy workload and endless paperwork, this workbook meets you where you are — no jargon, no judgment, just a practical starting point for understanding what you're going through.

Written by Fouzia Asim — educator and founder of Digital Fattom.
