About Digital Fattom

Digital Fattom is a blog about content creation, digital products, and the tools and platforms shaping how people create and sell online today. It's written and maintained by Fouzia Asim, a Karachi-based educator and content creator.

With over ten years of teaching experience in Chemistry, History, and Social Studies, I bring a research-driven, explain-it-clearly approach to everything I write — whether that's breaking down a new AI regulation, walking through how to sell your first digital product on Gumroad, or putting together a practical health and wellness guide.

What You'll Find Here

  • Guides on selling digital products and Canva templates on platforms like Gumroad and Etsy
  • Breakdowns of AI tools, platform trends, and what they mean for creators in Pakistan
  • Practical advice for teachers moving into online tutoring and freelancing
  • Health, wellness, and lifestyle guides grounded in credible research

Why "Digital Fattom"

Digital Fattom started as a way to document what I was learning while building a digital content business alongside a full-time teaching career — and it's grown into a resource for other creators, freelancers, and small business owners navigating the same path, especially here in Pakistan.
